How it works

  1. Download & install the app from this site.
  2. Grant Accessibility permission so it can read window text (the same feature screen readers use).
  3. Sign in with Google — for login only. We never read your email.
  4. Ask Claude. Your memory stays on your Mac; a secure tunnel lets Claude reach it to answer questions and set reminders.
Private by design. Your activity, text, and search index live only on your Mac. mitthuai's servers are a tunnel — they route Claude's questions to your Mac and the answers back. We don't store your memory and we don't read your email; Google is used solely to sign you in.
